# Nine Men's Morris

## Goal
Nine Men's Morris (in Vietnamese, cờ cối xay) is an old board game for two. Line up three of your pieces to form a mill, and each mill takes one of your opponent's pieces. Leave your opponent with only two pieces, or with no legal move, and you win.

## The board
The board is three squares, one inside the other, joined at the middles of their sides. Pieces stand on the 24 points where lines meet or end. A mill is three pieces of one colour on one straight line: along a side of a square, or across the joins. Each player has nine pieces; White moves first. White pieces are drawn outlined, Black pieces filled.

## Rules
1. Placing: in turn, each player puts a piece on any empty point until all nine are placed. The pieces still to place are shown above the board (Black) and below it (White).
2. Moving: then, in turn, each player moves one piece along a line to the next empty point.
3. Flying: a player down to three pieces may move a piece to any empty point. Flying can be turned off in the menu.

Whenever a move forms a new mill, that player takes one of the opponent's pieces off the board. A piece in a mill cannot be taken, unless all the opponent's pieces are in mills. Forming two mills at once still takes only one piece. Opening a mill and closing it again forms a new mill.

## End of the game
- A player left with two pieces loses.
- A player who cannot move on their turn loses.
- Draws: the same position for the third time, or 50 moves each (after all pieces are placed) without a mill.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. Your wins, losses and draws are counted for each level.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- You play White/Black, flying on/off: each select steps to the next choice. It applies to the next game; the game in progress keeps its sides and rules.
- Take back a move undoes your last move (and the device's reply).
- Resume continues the game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left, Right and the side buttons (Up, Down) move the cursor over the points, along the board's lines.
- Confirm places a piece on the cursor's point. In the moving phase, Confirm picks up a piece (its possible moves are ringed), then Confirm again on a ringed point moves it.
- After a mill, the pieces you may take are crossed: put the cursor on one and press Confirm.
- Back puts the picked piece down (or undoes the mill's move), or opens the menu. After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a point to place a piece; tap your piece, then its new point, to move. After a mill, tap a crossed piece to take it.
- Tap Menu for the menu.

## Tips
- While placing, do not rush to make mills: keep your pieces free to move. A player boxed in later loses.
- Points where four lines meet (the middles of the middle square) are the most flexible.
- Block your opponent's two-in-a-row before it becomes a mill.
- A "running mill" is strongest: a piece that steps out of one mill into another, closing a mill every move.
