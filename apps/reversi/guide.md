# Reversi

## Goal
Reversi is played with discs that are black on one side and white on the other. Black discs are drawn filled, white discs as rings. When neither player can move any more, whoever has more discs on the board wins.

## Rules
- The game starts with four discs in the centre, two of each colour. Black moves first.
- Place a disc so that it closes a straight line of your opponent's discs between it and another of your own discs: across, up and down or diagonally. Every such line is flipped to your colour, in all eight directions at once.
- A move must flip at least one disc. A square that flips nothing cannot be played.
- If you have no legal move, you pass and your opponent plays again. The line under the board tells you when someone passes.
- When neither side can move (usually because the board is full), the game ends and the discs are counted. Equal counts are a draw.
- The last disc played has a small square in its middle. The disc counts are at the top; the side to move has the thicker frame.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. Your wins, losses and draws against each level are shown under its button.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Options: choose your colour against the device (Black moves first) and whether the legal squares are shown as small dots.
- Resume continues an unfinished game; a game left open is kept when you exit the app.
- Take back a move returns to your previous turn.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- With Show moves on, Left and Right jump the cursor to the previous or next legal square, and Up and Down to the nearest legal square in a row above or below. The cursor never stops on a square you cannot play.
- With Show moves off, the arrows move the cursor freely over the board, so you have to find the legal squares yourself.
- Confirm plays on the cursor square. Back opens the menu.
- When a game is over, Confirm starts a new one at the same level.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a square to play there.
- Tap Menu (top right) for the menu.
- After a game ends, tap the board to play again.

## Tips for beginners
- Corners can never be flipped. Take them when you can.
- Avoid the squares right next to an empty corner, especially the diagonal one: they often hand the corner to your opponent.
- Flipping many discs early is rarely good. Keep few discs and many possible moves, and try to leave your opponent with few.
- Edges are strong, but only when your opponent cannot squeeze in behind them.
- Count carefully near the end: the last few moves decide the score.
