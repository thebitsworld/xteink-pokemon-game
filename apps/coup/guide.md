# Co Up (Cờ úp)

## Goal
Cờ úp is xiangqi (Chinese chess) played with hidden pieces. As in xiangqi, checkmate the opposing general. Red moves first. Nobody knows what the face-down pieces are, not even their owner.

## Setting up
- Only the two generals start face up, on their usual points in the palace.
- Each side's other 15 pieces (2 advisors, 2 elephants, 2 horses, 2 chariots, 2 cannons, 5 soldiers) are shuffled and placed face down on the usual starting points of those pieces.

## Face-down pieces
- A face-down piece (a disc with a criss-cross pattern) moves as the piece whose starting point it stands on: on a chariot point like a chariot, on a horse point like a horse, and so on. Pick it up to see its moves marked.
- After its first move it is turned face up, and from then on it moves as what it really is.
- A face-down piece may capture or give check as its point's piece. Capturing a face-down piece shows what it was.

## Face-up pieces
- General, horse, chariot, cannon and soldier move as in xiangqi (the generals may not face each other on an open file).
- Advisors and elephants are free, as usual in cờ úp. An advisor steps one point diagonally anywhere on the board. An elephant moves two points diagonally anywhere, may cross the river, and is still blocked if the point in between is taken.
- A soldier that turns up on your own half moves only forward until it crosses the river.

## End of the game
- A side with no legal move loses: checkmate and stalemate alike.
- Draws: the same position three times, 60 moves each without a capture or a piece turned up, or only the two generals left.

## Notation
Under the board, K A E H R C P name the pieces. "?a1-a3=H": a face-down piece turned up as a horse. "Ra1xa10[C]": a face-down cannon was captured.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. The device plays fair: it knows only what you see, and values each face-down piece as the average of those still hidden.
- "You play Red/Black" swaps your side for the next new game (as Black the board is turned).
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Take back a move undoes your last move and the device's reply. Resume continues the game, which is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- The arrows move the cursor. Confirm picks up a piece, then Confirm on a marked point moves it.
- Back drops the piece or opens the menu. After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap your piece, then tap where it should go. Tap Menu for the menu.

## Tips
- Count what has turned up. There are two chariots, two cannons and two horses on each side: once both chariots are known, no hidden piece can be one.
- Face-down pieces on the cannon points can capture over a screen from the very first move, so watch the cannon lines early.
- Every face-down piece is a gamble: keep a few near your general.
