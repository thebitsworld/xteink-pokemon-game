# Blackjack

## Goal
Beat the dealer: finish closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

## Rules
- Cards 2-10 count their number, J, Q and K count 10, an ace counts 11 or 1, whichever is better.
- You start with $50 (Bank, top right). Choose a bet, then you and the dealer get two cards; one dealer card stays face down.
- Hit takes another card. Stand keeps your total. Over 21 is a bust and loses at once.
- The dealer then draws until reaching 17 or more, then stands.
- Higher total wins and pays even money. Equal totals are a push: your bet comes back.
- Blackjack (21 with your first two cards) pays 3 to 2. If you or the dealer has 21 from the deal, the round ends at once.
- Double: on your first two cards, double the bet, take exactly one card and stand.
- Split: two cards of the same rank (two 8s, two kings) become two hands with the same bet each, up to four hands. A 21 on a split hand is not a blackjack.
- Insurance: when the dealer shows an ace, you may pay half your bet; it pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack.
- The game uses four decks. Your bank is kept between sessions; if it runs out you get $50 again.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Betting: Left/Right lower or raise the bet by $5, Confirm deals, Back exits.
- Your turn: Left hits, Right stands, Confirm doubles (when shown). When SPLIT appears, either side button splits.
- Insurance: Confirm or Left insures, Back or Right declines.
- After the round: Confirm for Next (or the next split hand).

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Use the button bar at the bottom: bet - and +, Deal, Hit, Stand, Double, Next, Exit.
- Tap the SPLIT box to split. For insurance, tap the left half of the box to insure, the right half to decline.

## Tips
- Leaving in the middle of a hand loses the bet on the table.
- Stand on 17 or more; hit on 11 or less. Double on 10 or 11 when the dealer shows a low card.
- Always split aces and 8s; never split 10s.
