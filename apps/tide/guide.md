# Tide & Paper

## Goal

Collect pairs and sets of sea cards, then end the round at the right moment. Rounds add up; the first player to reach 40 points wins the game (if both pass 40, the higher score wins). Four Mermaids in your cards win the game at once.

## A turn

1. Draw: either draw two cards from the Deck, keep one and put the other face up on a discard pile, or take the top card of Pile 1 or Pile 2. If a pile is empty, the card you do not keep must go there.
2. Play pairs (optional): pick two cards that form a pair and press Pair. You may play several.
3. End: press End turn - or, with 7 or more points in your cards, Stop or Last chance.

## Pairs

Pairs score 1 point each whether they are in your hand or played. Play one to use its effect:

- Crab + Crab: take any card from either discard pile.
- Boat + Boat: you play another turn.
- Fish + Fish: draw the top card of the Deck.
- Swimmer + Shark: steal a random card from the device's hand.

Two Swimmers or two Sharks are not a pair.

## Sets and bonus cards

- Shell: 1-6 shells score 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.
- Octopus: 1-5 score 0, 3, 6, 9, 12.
- Penguin: 1-3 score 1, 3, 5.
- Sailor: 1-2 score 0, 5.
- Beacon: +1 per Boat. Shoal: +1 per Fish. Colony: +2 per Penguin. Captain: +3 per Sailor.
- Mermaid: each one scores as many points as you have cards of one mark - the first your most common mark, the second the next one, and so on.

Every card except a Mermaid has a mark in its top-left corner: a circle, square or triangle, filled or hollow. Your mark bonus is how many cards you hold of your most common mark. Selecting one card in your hand shows what it does.

## Ending a round

With 7+ points in your cards you may end the round:

- Stop: the round ends now. Both players score their card points (no mark bonus).
- Last chance: the device gets one more turn. Then, if you have more points than it, you score your cards plus your mark bonus and it scores only its mark bonus. If not (a tie counts as not), you score only your mark bonus and it scores its cards.

If a player has to draw and the Deck is empty, the round ends and nobody scores.

## Controls

Buttons (X3/X4): the side buttons Up/Down move between the table (Deck and piles), your hand and the buttons; Left/Right move along a row; Confirm picks; Back opens the menu. To keep a drawn card, pick it, then pick the pile for the other one.

Touch (X4 Pro): tap the Deck or a pile, tap two hand cards, then tap Pair, End turn, Stop or Last chance. Menu is at the top right. Between rounds, tap (or Confirm) to go on.

## Tips

- Bet Last chance only when you are clearly ahead: a failed bet hands the device its whole hand.
- Stop early when the device is building a big set.
- Keep an eye on the discard piles - a Crab pair can fetch the card you need.
- Mermaids are strong late, when you have many cards of one mark.
