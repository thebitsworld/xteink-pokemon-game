# Hearts

## Goal
A trick-taking card game for four: you (South, at the bottom) against West, North and East, played by the device. Avoid taking penalty points. The game ends when someone reaches 100 points, and the lowest score wins.

## Points
- Every heart you take costs 1 point.
- The queen of spades costs 13 points.
- All other cards are worth nothing. Each hand has 26 points in all.
- Shooting the moon: if one player takes all 26 points in a hand, that player scores 0 and the other three get 26 each.

## Passing
Before each hand you pick three cards to give away: to the left, then to the right, then across, then no passing, and round again. You receive three cards at the same time.

## Playing a hand
- The player holding the two of clubs leads it to the first trick.
- Play goes clockwise. You must follow the suit that was led if you can. If you cannot, play any card.
- The highest card of the suit led wins the trick (ace is high) and leads the next one. There are no trumps.
- No points on the first trick: hearts and the queen of spades may not be played on it, unless you hold nothing else.
- Hearts may not be led until a heart has been played on another suit ("hearts are broken"), unless you hold only hearts.
- Cards you may not play are greyed. If you pick one anyway, the status line says why.
- Beside each name is the game score, with the points taken this hand in brackets.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move along your hand; the side buttons (Up/Down) jump between its two rows.
- When passing: Confirm picks or un-picks a card (it rises). After three cards, move right past the last card to the Pass button and press Confirm.
- When playing: Confirm plays the selected card.
- After a hand, Confirm deals the next one. Back opens the menu.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap cards to pick them for passing, then tap the Pass button. Tap a card to play it.
- After a hand, tap to continue. Tap Menu for the menu.

## Starting a game
New game: Easy or Normal players sets how well the device plays. Resume continues a game; it is kept when you exit.

## Tips for beginners
- Pass away high spades (ace, king, queen) and high hearts.
- Try to get rid of a whole suit early (void it): then you can throw away bad cards when that suit is led.
- Play low cards under a trick you do not want to win.
- Keep an eye on who has points. If one player is taking everything, take one heart to stop them shooting the moon.
