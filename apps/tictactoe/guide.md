# Tic-Tac-Toe

## Goal
Get three of your marks in a row on the 3x3 grid: across, down or diagonally.

## Rules
- Players take turns putting their mark (X or O) in an empty square.
- X always moves first.
- The first to complete a line of three wins; the line is drawn through it.
- If all nine squares fill with no line, the game is a draw.
- Against the device, the starting side alternates: you start one game as X, the device starts the next and you play O, and so on.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy (often plays at random), Normal (always takes a win or blocks yours, otherwise mostly plays well) or Hard (perfect play: it never loses). Wins, losses and draws are counted for each level.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader; X starts.
- Resume returns to an unfinished game while the app is open.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the cursor along a row; Up/Down (the side buttons) move it between rows.
- Confirm plays in the framed square.
- Back opens the menu; Back in the menu exits.
- After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap an empty square to play there.
- Tap Menu for the menu. After a game, tap the board to play again.

## Tips
- Starting in the centre or a corner gives the most chances.
- Always check whether your opponent has two in a line, and block it.
- Two lines of two at the same time win: the opponent can only block one.
- Against Hard, a draw is the best result.
