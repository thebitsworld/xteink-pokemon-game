# Checkers

## Goal
Capture or block all of your opponent's pieces. You win when your opponent has no legal move left. These are the English draughts rules on an 8x8 board.

## Rules
- Play uses only the dark (shaded) squares. Each side starts with 12 pieces on its first three rows.
- White (the light pieces, at the bottom) moves first. Against the device you are always White.
- A man moves one square diagonally forward.
- Capture by jumping diagonally over an enemy piece next to you onto the empty square behind it. The jumped piece is removed.
- Capturing is compulsory. If any capture is possible, you must make one (you may choose which).
- If the jumping piece can jump again, it must keep going: the whole chain is one move.
- A man that reaches the far row becomes a king (a ring is drawn on it). A king moves and jumps one square diagonally in any direction. A man crowned during a chain stops there.
- Draw: 40 moves each (80 in total) with no capture and no crowning.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard; wins, losses and draws are counted per level.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Resume continues an unfinished game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right and the side buttons (Up/Down) move the cursor.
- Pieces that can move are marked with a dot. When a capture is compulsory the status line says "you must capture" and only those pieces are marked.
- Confirm picks a piece (Pick); circles show where it can land. Move the cursor there and Confirm (Move). For a chain of jumps, confirm each landing square in turn.
- Back drops the picked piece, or opens the menu. After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap your piece, then tap the landing square (each one in turn for a chain).
- Tap Menu for the menu; after a game, tap anywhere to play again.

## Tips for beginners
- Keep your back row filled as long as you can: it stops the opponent from crowning.
- Move in groups; a lone piece is easy to trap.
- Because captures are forced, you can offer one piece to pull an enemy piece into a double jump for you.
- Pieces on the side edges cannot be jumped, but they also do less.
- Get a king early in the endgame: kings are much stronger than men.
