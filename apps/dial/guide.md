# Mind Dial

## Goal

A party game for three or more players around one reader, played as a team. Each round a card names the two ends of a scale, such as Cold - Hot, and the reader hides a target somewhere along it. One player sees the target and gives a clue; everyone else tries to put the pointer right on it. Score as many points as you can together.

## A round

1. Pass the reader to this round's clue giver and press "I'm the clue giver". Everyone else looks away.
2. The clue giver sees the scale with the target on it. They think of something that sits at that spot on the scale - for Cold - Hot, a target a little past the middle might be "a cup of tea" - and say it out loud. Then they press "Hide the target".
3. The group talks it over and moves the pointer to where they think the clue sits, then presses "Lock it in".
4. The target is revealed and the points are added.

Take turns being the clue giver from round to round.

## Scoring

The target has three bands. The scale runs from 0 to 100, and the pointer earns:

- 4 points within 4 of the target's centre (the black band),
- 3 points within 12,
- 2 points within 20,
- nothing further away.

A game is 5, 7 or 10 rounds, so the best possible score is 4 points a round. The menu keeps your best score for each length.

## Controls

Buttons (X3/X4): when guessing, Left/Right move the pointer a little and the side buttons Up/Down move it a lot (Up towards the left end, Down towards the right). Confirm presses the big button at the bottom. Back opens the menu (Resume continues the game).

Touch (X4 Pro): when guessing, tap the scale where the clue sits; then tap the big button at the bottom.

## Tips

- Clue giver: pick something everyone knows, and think about how the others will judge it, not only yourself.
- Avoid clues that are exactly at an end ("the Sun" for Hot) unless the target is at the very end.
- Group: talk about it before locking in, and say why - "warmer than a bath, colder than a sauna".
