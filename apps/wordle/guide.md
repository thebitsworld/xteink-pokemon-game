# Wordle

## Goal
Guess the secret five-letter English word in six tries or fewer.

## Rules
- Type a five-letter word and submit it with ok. Each letter tile then shows how close you are:
- Black tile with white letter: right letter in the right place.
- Grey (dotted) tile: the letter is in the word, but in another place.
- Plain white tile: the letter is not in the word (or not as many times as you used it).
- Letters that are not in the word are greyed on the keyboard.
- Any five letters are accepted; the game does not check against a dictionary.
- Guess the word to win (WINNER!). After six wrong guesses the game shows the word.
- An unfinished game is kept when you exit.

## Keyboard
- Three rows of letters, then: <- deletes the last letter, ok submits, clear empties the row, and reset (below the keyboard) asks to start a new word.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- The side buttons move the selection left and right along a keyboard row.
- Left/Right move it up and down between rows (the hints say Up, Down).
- Confirm presses the selected key. After the fifth letter the selection jumps to ok.
- Back exits. After a game, Confirm plays a new word.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap the keys directly. The bar at the bottom has Back, Select, Up and Down.
- After a game, tap any key (or Play) for a new word.

## Tips
- Start with a word full of common letters, such as CRANE or SLATE.
- Use the clues: keep black letters in place, move grey ones, and avoid greyed keys.
- A letter can appear twice: if you are stuck, try a double letter.
