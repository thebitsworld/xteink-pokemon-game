# Block Drop

## Goal
Falling-block puzzle: steer and turn the falling pieces so they fill complete rows. Full rows disappear. Keep going as long as you can for a high score.

## Rules
- The well is 10 squares wide and 20 high. Pieces of four squares (seven shapes) fall one row at a time; the next one is shown at the right.
- A piece locks when it cannot fall any further. Every row that is completely filled is cleared and the rows above drop down.
- Score for rows cleared at once: 1 row 100, 2 rows 300, 3 rows 500, 4 rows 800, each times the level. Dropping a piece faster adds 1 point per row.
- Every 10 rows clears the level goes up and pieces fall faster.
- The game is over when a new piece has no room at the top.
- An unfinished game is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the piece sideways.
- Up (side button) turns the piece; it shifts a square away from a wall if needed.
- Hold Confirm (Drop) to make the piece fall fast.
- Down (side button) asks to reset the game: Confirm starts over, Back cancels.
- Back exits. After a game over, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap the top third of the screen to turn the piece.
- Tap the left or right half of the middle to move it.
- Tap the bottom third (or Drop in the bottom bar) to move it down one row.
- The bottom bar also has Back, Left and Right.

## Tips
- Keep the surface flat, and leave one column at the edge open for the long piece: four rows at once scores the most.
- Look at the next piece before you place the current one.
- Avoid covering gaps; a hole under a block is hard to clear.
