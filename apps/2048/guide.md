# 2048

## Goal
Slide numbered tiles on a 4x4 grid and merge equal ones to build a 2048 tile, then keep going for the highest score you can.

## Rules
- Each move slides every tile as far as it goes in one direction: up, down, left or right.
- Two tiles with the same number that run into each other merge into one tile with their sum (2+2 makes 4, 4+4 makes 8). A tile merges only once per move.
- Every merge adds the new tile's value to your score.
- After each move that changes the grid, a new tile appears in an empty square: usually a 2, sometimes a 4.
- The game is over when the grid is full and no two neighbouring tiles match. The header then shows GAME OVER.
- Reaching 2048 does not end the game; tiles of 2048 and more are drawn black.
- Your board and score are kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left slides up, Right slides down (as the hints say: Up, Down).
- The side buttons: Up slides left, Down slides right.
- Confirm asks to reset the game; Confirm again to start over, Back to cancel.
- Back exits.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Swipe up, down, left or right to slide the tiles.
- The bar at the bottom has Back (exit), Reset, Up and Down.

## Tips
- Keep your biggest tile in one corner and build along the edge next to it.
- Use mostly two or three directions; avoid the move that pulls the big tile out of its corner.
- Keep one row full so the big tiles cannot be pushed around.
