# Cult Ledger

## Goal

A solo card game. You run a small cult in a quiet town. Gather enough relics, members and a prisoner to offer, then summon your god - before the police raid you, your members walk out, or the bishop's inspector arrives.

## Your resources

The top of the screen shows Coin, Food, Cultists (members), Prisoners, Relics and Heat (how suspicious the town is). Relics, Cultists and Prisoners show what the summoning needs, for example "Relics 1/3".

- Easy: start with 3 Coin, 4 Food, 3 Cultists, 0 Heat. The summoning needs 3 Relics, 4 Cultists, 1 Prisoner. Gossip every 6 turns; the inspector arrives on turn 20.
- Hard: start with 2 Coin, 3 Food, 2 Cultists, 1 Heat. It needs 4 Relics, 4 Cultists, 1 Prisoner. Gossip every 4 turns; the inspector arrives on turn 25.

## A turn

Three event cards come up, each with two choices. Pick one choice on one card; its effect happens at once (for example "-1 Coin, +1 Cultists"). You must be able to pay what a choice costs; choices you cannot pay for have a thin frame. Heat may always go up. If nothing on the table can be paid for, the button under the cards lets you lie low instead (+1 Heat).

The cards you did not pick go back under the deck, so every event comes round before any repeats.

## Between turns

- Feast: every 4th turn the members eat one Food for every two Cultists. If the food runs short, that many members leave.
- Gossip: every few turns the town talks, +1 Heat.
- The line under the resources says how many turns until the next feast and gossip.

## Winning and losing

- From turn 6, once you have everything the summoning needs, a "Summon your god!" button appears under the cards. Press it to win at once; it costs nothing.
- Heat 5 or more at the end of a turn: the police raid you. You lose.
- No Cultists left: the cult is over. You lose.
- The inspector arrives at the end of the last turn (20 on Easy, 25 on Hard). Summon before you play that turn's card.

## Controls

Buttons (X3/X4): the side buttons Up/Down move between the three cards and the button under them; Left/Right switch between a card's two choices; Confirm picks; Back opens the menu.

Touch (X4 Pro): tap a choice, or the button under the cards.

## Tips

- Heat is the real clock. Keep a way to lower it (paying a fine, the lawyer, a quiet week) for when it reaches 3 or 4.
- Before a feast, check you have half as much Food as Cultists.
- Coin buys relics and calm; do not run out of it.
- One Prisoner is enough: do not take on extra heat for a second one.
