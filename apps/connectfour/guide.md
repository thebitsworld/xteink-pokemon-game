# Connect Four

## Goal
Be the first to line up four of your discs in a row: across, up and down, or diagonally.

## Rules
- The board has 7 columns and 6 rows. On your turn you drop one disc into a column that is not full; it falls to the lowest free place.
- Players take turns. The first to make a line of four wins, and the winning discs are marked.
- If the board fills up with no line of four, the game is a draw.
- Solid discs move first. Against the device you are always the solid discs and you always start. With two players, Black (solid) starts and White (ring with a dot) follows.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. Your wins, losses and draws are counted for each level.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Resume returns to an unfinished game while the app is open.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right (or the side buttons) move the marker over the columns.
- Confirm drops a disc in the marked column.
- Back opens the menu; Back in the menu exits.
- After a game, Confirm plays again at the same level.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a column to drop a disc there.
- Tap Menu for the menu. After a game, tap the board to play again.

## Tips
- The middle column is part of the most lines: take it early.
- Before every move, check whether the opponent can make four next turn, and block it.
- Look for a double threat: two places where you would win at once. The opponent can only block one.
