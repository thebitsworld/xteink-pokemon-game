# Backgammon

## Goal
Bring all 15 of your checkers into your home board, then bear them off. The first side with all 15 off wins.

## The board
The board is turned a quarter: 13 to 24 run down the left side, 12 to 1 down the right, and the bar crosses the middle.
- You play White (outlined checkers) and move from 24 to 1: up the left side, across the top, down the right. Your home board is 1 to 6 at the bottom right, with your tray below it.
- Black (filled) moves the other way; its home is 19 to 24 at the bottom left.
- A stack of more than five shows its count. The trays show the checkers off and the pip count (pips still to travel).
- The last move is marked on the point numbers: framed where checkers left, inverted where they landed.

## Rules
1. The dice are rolled for you each turn. Each die moves one checker that many points; doubles are played four times.
2. You may land on an empty point, on your own checkers, or on a single opposing checker (a blot), never on two or more.
3. Landing on a blot hits it: it goes to the bar and must come back in, into the opponent's home board, before that side moves anything else.
4. Use both dice if you can. If only one can be used, use the larger one when possible.
5. With all 15 in your home board you bear off: a die takes a checker off its point. A higher die may take one from your highest point when none is further back.
6. The first game starts with one die each; the higher moves first with both numbers.

## Scoring
- A win scores 1 point, a gammon 2 (the loser has borne off nothing), a backgammon 3 (and still has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board).
- The menu keeps the match points for each level and for two players; New match sets them back to zero. There is no doubling cube.

## Levels
- Easy plays a random move but likes to hit.
- Normal weighs every move: pips, blots and the shots at them, made points, primes, anchors and the home board.
- Hard also checks the best answer to each of the opponent's 21 possible rolls.
- Two players take turns on one reader. Resume continues a game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the frame over the checkers that can move; Confirm picks one.
- Its landing points get a dot: Left/Right choose one (one die, or both dice with the same checker), Confirm moves.
- When the dice are used, Confirm ends the turn (Pass when nothing could move).
- Back drops a picked checker, takes back this turn's steps one by one, then opens the menu.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a point (or the bar), then where the checker goes; tap your tray to bear off.
- Undo takes back a step, Done ends the turn, Menu opens the menu.

## Tips
- Make points (two or more checkers), above all in your home board and on your 7 point.
- Do not leave blots where many rolls hit them.
- Six points in a row (a prime) cannot be passed.
- Well ahead in the race? Break contact and run home.
