# Hexland

## Goal

Settle a hex island against three device players, Ana, Ben and Cyd: gather resources, trade and build. The first to reach 10 victory points on their own turn wins.

## The island

Each of the 19 hexes yields a resource, shown by name and mark: Wood (trees), Brick (wall), Wool (fleece), Grain (ears) and Ore (mountains). The Desert yields nothing. The other hexes carry a number from 2 to 12; its dots show how often it comes up, and 6 and 8, the likeliest, are bold with a double ring. On a random island no 6 or 8 touches another. The menu also offers a fixed Starter island.

Nine ports lie around the coast. A 3:1 port lets you trade any 3 of one resource for 1; a 2:1 port does the same with 2 of its own resource. You use a port once you have a settlement or city on one of its two corners.

## Pieces

You have 15 roads, 5 settlements and 4 cities. Your pieces are solid black; Ana's are outlined, Ben's hatched and Cyd's dotted. Roads run along edges, settlements and cities stand on corners.

- Road: 1 Wood, 1 Brick.
- Settlement (1 point): 1 Wood, 1 Brick, 1 Wool, 1 Grain.
- City (2 points): 2 Grain, 3 Ore. It replaces one of your settlements.
- Development card: 1 Wool, 1 Grain, 1 Ore.

Distance rule: a settlement can never stand on a corner next to another settlement or city, whoever owns it. After the set-up, a new settlement must also touch one of your roads, and a new road must join your roads, settlements or cities. You cannot build a road through another player's settlement.

## Set-up

In turn order each player places a settlement and a road next to it; then, in reverse order, a second settlement and road. Your second settlement gives you one of each resource around it. Whoever goes first is drawn at random.

## A turn

1. Roll: both dice are rolled. Every hex with that number pays its resource: 1 for each settlement and 2 for each city on its corners. The log shows the roll and what everyone received. If the bank runs short of a resource, only a single player can be paid what is left; with several, nobody gets it.
2. Build and trade, as often as you like and can afford.
3. End your turn.

## Rolling a 7

Nobody is paid. Everyone holding more than 7 cards discards half of them (rounded down); you pick yours. Then the player who rolled moves the robber to another hex and takes a random card from one player with a building there. The hex under the robber pays nothing until it moves.

## Development cards

Draw one with Build. You may play one card a turn, but not one bought this turn. Knights may be played before you roll.

- Knight (14): move the robber and take a card.
- Road Building (2): place two free roads.
- Year of Plenty (2): take any two resources from the bank.
- Monopoly (2): every other player gives you all their cards of one resource.
- Victory Point (5): kept hidden in your hand, 1 point each.

## Bonus points

- Longest Road (2 points): the first to have an unbroken road of at least 5 roads. Another player takes it with a longer one; a tie keeps it with its holder. A settlement built on a corner of a road breaks it. If the holder's road is broken and others tie for the longest, nobody holds it.
- Largest Army (2 points): the first to play 3 knights; another player takes it by playing more.

## Trading

- With the bank: give 4 of one resource for 1 of any other, or 3 or 2 at your ports. The rate for each resource is shown in your hand.
- Offer to players: offer 1 or 2 of one resource for 1 of another, to one player or to all. A device player accepts only when the trade helps it, and nobody deals with a player close to winning.
- Device players sometimes offer you a trade on their turn; accept or decline.

## The screen

Below the island: the four players (points, cards, Dev development cards, K knights played, R road length; LR and LA mark Longest Road and Largest Army), the log, your hand with each trade rate, and the buttons Roll, Build, Trade, Cards and End.

## Controls

Touch (X4 Pro): tap a button, then tap the corner, edge or hex where a piece or the robber goes; small rings mark the allowed places. Tap a pop-up item to choose it; tap outside a pop-up to close it. Menu is at the top right.

Buttons: Left and Right move along the buttons, the allowed places or a pop-up's items; Up and Down jump to the row of places above or below. Confirm picks, Back cancels or opens the menu. During Road Building, Back stops after the first road.

## Levels and records

Normal device players place with some randomness and trade freely; Hard ones aim the robber at the leader, chase the longest road and trade more carefully. The menu keeps your wins and average place per level. A game in progress is saved when you leave.

## Tips

- Settle on corners with high numbers and a mix of resources; Brick and Wood matter early, Grain and Ore later.
- A port that matches what you produce most turns surplus into anything.
- Keep 7 cards or fewer, and turn Ore into cities: they double your income.
