# Hex

## Goal
Hex is played on a rhombus of hexagons. Black owns the top and bottom edges (drawn as solid lines), White owns the left and right edges (drawn as dotted lines). Join your two edges with an unbroken chain of your stones before your opponent joins theirs.

## Rules
- Black moves first. Players take turns placing one stone on any empty cell.
- Stones never move and are never captured.
- Each cell touches six neighbours. A chain is a line of your stones that touch each other.
- The first player to link their two edges wins. A game of Hex can never end in a draw: when the board fills up, exactly one player has a chain.
- The last stone played is marked with a small dot.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. Your colour alternates every new game, so sometimes you start as Black and sometimes the device does. The status line tells you your colour and your edges.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Board: choose 7x7, 9x9 or 11x11 for the next game. Bigger boards make longer, deeper games.
- Resume continues an unfinished game. A game left open is kept when you exit the app.
- Your wins and losses against each level are shown under its button.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the cursor along a row; the side buttons (Up/Down) move it between rows. The cursor wraps around the edges.
- Confirm places a stone on the cursor cell.
- Back opens the menu. In the menu, Back exits the app.
- When a game is over, Confirm starts a new one at the same level.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a cell to place your stone.
- Tap Menu (top right) for the menu.
- After a game ends, tap anywhere to play again.

## Tips for beginners
- Play near the centre early: a central stone helps toward both of your edges.
- The bridge: two of your stones with two empty cells between them that both touch each stone are as good as connected. If your opponent fills one of those cells, take the other.
- Blocking right next to an enemy stone is weak; block from a little distance instead.
- Attack and defence are the same thing in Hex: every chain you build also gets in your opponent's way.
- Start on 7x7 against Easy, then move up.
