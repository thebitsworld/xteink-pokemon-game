# Codex: Ink & Iron

## Goal
A roguelike deckbuilder. Fight your way through 15 pages with a deck of cards and defeat the boss on page 15. If your HP reaches 0, the run is over.

## Rules
- You start with 50 HP, 50 gold, a 10-card deck and the Silver Quill relic.
- Each turn you get your Ink back and draw 4 cards. Playing a card costs the Ink shown in its corner.
- Attack cards damage the enemy; its Ward absorbs damage first. Ward cards give you Ward, which absorbs the enemy's hits.
- Back ends your turn: your hand is discarded, the enemy acts, and your Ward is halved.
- The enemy's Intent shows its next move: Attack (damage) or Defend (it gains Ward).
- Couplet: each card is a Blade, Ward or Script card. Some cards get a bonus when the card played just before them, this turn, was of a certain type. The bonus box then reads COUPLET READY. Rhythm shows the type of the last card played.
- Illumination cards are Powers: they last the whole fight, once per fight.
- Winning a fight gives 18 gold and a choice of one of three new cards (or skip).
- Pages 3, 7, 9 and 13 are events with choices; pages 5 and 10 are shops (cards, relics, +12 HP); pages 8, 11 and 14 have elite enemies.
- The run is saved as you go.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Fight: Left/Right choose a card, Confirm plays it, Back ends the turn.
- Side buttons in a fight: Up shows your deck, Down your discard pile. There, the side buttons or Confirm switch tabs (Deck, Disc, Relics, Menu), Left/Right turn pages, Back returns. The Menu tab has Resume, Save & Title, Exit and Abandon.
- Rewards, events and the shop: Left/Right choose, Confirm takes or buys, Back skips or leaves the shop.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a card to select it, tap it again to play it. The bottom bar has End Turn, Play, < and >.
- Tap menu entries, rewards, shop items and event choices directly.

## Tips
- Plan the order of your cards each turn to trigger Couplets.
- Ward is halved every turn, so build it just before a big attack.
- Skipping a weak card reward keeps your deck focused.
