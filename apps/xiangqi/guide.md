# Xiangqi

## Goal
Xiangqi is Chinese chess. Checkmate the opposing general: attack it so that it has no safe move. Red moves first.

## The board
Pieces stand on the points where lines cross, not inside squares. The river across the middle divides the two sides. Each side has a palace: the 3x3 box with diagonal lines at its end of the board.

## The pieces
Each piece shows its Chinese character, as on a real set. Move notation under the board uses the letters K A E H R C P.
- General (K): one step along a line, and never leaves the palace. The two generals may not face each other on an open file.
- Advisor (A): one step diagonally, inside the palace.
- Elephant (E): exactly two steps diagonally. It cannot cross the river and is blocked if the point in between is taken.
- Horse (H): one step along a line, then one diagonally outward. It is blocked if the first point (its "leg") is taken.
- Chariot (R): any distance along a line, like a chess rook.
- Cannon (C): moves like a chariot, but captures only by jumping over exactly one piece (of either side).
- Soldier (P): one step forward. After crossing the river it may also step sideways. It never moves back.

## End of the game
- A side with no legal move loses. That covers checkmate and stalemate alike (in xiangqi, stalemate is a loss).
- Draws: the same position three times, 60 moves each without a capture, or no horse, chariot, cannon or soldier left on either side.
- You may not make a move that leaves your own general in check.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. "You play Red/Black" swaps your side; as Black the board is turned so your pieces are at the bottom.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Take back a move undoes your last move (and the device's reply).
- Resume continues the game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right and the side buttons (Up/Down) move the cursor over the points.
- Confirm picks up a piece (its possible moves are marked), then Confirm again on a marked point to move.
- Back drops the picked piece, or opens the menu. After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap your piece, then tap where it should go. Tap Menu for the menu.

## Tips for beginners
- Get your chariots out early: they are your strongest pieces.
- A cannon needs a screen to capture; it is strongest early, while the board is full.
- Keep advisors and elephants home to guard the general.
- A horse whose leg is blocked cannot move that way, so watch which points are free.
