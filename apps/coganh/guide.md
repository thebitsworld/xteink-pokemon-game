# Co Ganh

## Goal
Cờ gánh is a Vietnamese folk board game for two, from Quảng Nam. Pieces are never taken off the board: they change sides. Trap your opponent's pieces until every one of the 16 pieces is yours.

## The board
The board has 25 points in a 5 by 5 grid. Every point is joined to its neighbours across and up and down. Half the points, the strong ones (the corners, the centre and every other point between them), also have diagonal lines: the two long diagonals and a diamond through the middles of the sides. Pieces only move along the lines.

Each player has 8 pieces on the outer edge: White on the bottom row, both ends of the second row from the bottom and the left end of the middle row; Black the same from the top (the right end of the middle row). White pieces are drawn outlined, Black pieces filled. White moves first.

## Rules
1. On your turn, move one of your pieces one step along a line to an empty point next to it.
2. Gánh (carry): if the point you move to lies between two of your opponent's pieces on one line, both change to your colour. Every line through the point counts, so one move can win 2, 4 or 6 pieces.
3. Vây (surround): after your move, any group of your opponent's pieces (pieces joined by lines) with no empty point next to it changes to your colour. A single piece is a group of one.
4. Gánh comes first, then vây. Moving in between two enemy pieces yourself is safe, and your own move never turns your own pieces.
5. You win when your opponent has no pieces left.

## Forced gánh (mở)
When a player moves a piece away and leaves an empty point between two of their own pieces, the opponent can step in and gánh them. Some players play that the opponent must then do it. This rule is off by default; turn it on in the menu. With it on, if your opponent has just left a point where you can gánh, your only legal moves are into that point. Players use it to set traps.

## Draws
- The same position, with the same player to move, for the third time.
- 50 moves each in which no piece changes colour.
- 150 moves each in the whole game.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. Your wins, losses and draws are counted for each level.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- You play first/second, forced gánh on/off: each select steps to the next choice. It applies to the next game. Playing second against the device, the board is turned round so your pieces are at the bottom.
- Take back a move undoes your last move (and the device's reply).
- Resume continues the game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left and Right move the cursor along the rows, running on into the next row. The side buttons move it up and down.
- Confirm picks up a piece; the points it can move to are ringed. Confirm on a ringed point moves it there.
- Back puts the picked piece down, or opens the menu. After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap one of your pieces, then a ringed point. Tap Menu for the menu.

## Reading the board
The last move shows a small square where the piece came from and a box where it went. Every piece it turned is ringed, and the line under the board says how many were won by gánh and by vây.

## Tips
- Strong points with diagonals give a piece eight directions: hold the centre.
- Never leave an empty point between two of your pieces that an enemy piece can reach.
- Keep your pieces with room to move: a crowded group in a corner is easy to surround.
- With few pieces left, the opponent is won by shrinking its room, not by chasing.
