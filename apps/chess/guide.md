# Chess

## Goal
Checkmate the opposing king: attack it so that it has no way to escape. White moves first. The app plays the full rules of chess.

## The pieces
- King: one square in any direction. It may never move into check.
- Queen: any distance in a straight line or diagonally.
- Rook: any distance in a straight line.
- Bishop: any distance diagonally.
- Knight: an L-shape (two squares one way, one square sideways). It jumps over pieces.
- Pawn: one square forward, or two from its starting square. It captures one square diagonally forward.

## Special moves
- Castling: the king moves two squares toward a rook and the rook jumps over it. Allowed only if neither has moved, the squares between are empty, and the king is not in check and does not pass through or land on an attacked square. Pick the king and choose the square two steps away.
- En passant: a pawn that just moved two squares can be captured by an enemy pawn beside it, as if it had moved only one.
- Promotion: a pawn reaching the last rank becomes a Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight; the app asks which.

## End of the game
- Checkmate wins. You may never leave your own king in check.
- Draws: stalemate (no legal move while not in check), fifty moves each without a capture or pawn move, the same position three times, or not enough material to mate (kings alone, or king and one bishop or knight against a king).

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. "You play White/Black" swaps your side; as Black the board is turned so your pieces are at the bottom.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Take back a move undoes your last move (and the device's reply); then choose Resume.
- Resume continues the game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right and the side buttons (Up/Down) move the cursor.
- Confirm picks a piece (Pick); dots and circles show where it can go. Move the cursor there and Confirm (Move).
- For a promotion, Left/Right choose the piece and Confirm takes it.
- Back drops the picked piece, or opens the menu. After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap your piece, then tap the square to move to. For a promotion, tap the piece you want. Tap Menu for the menu.

## Tips for beginners
- Control the centre with pawns and bring knights and bishops out early.
- Castle early to keep your king safe.
- Do not move the queen out too soon: it gets chased around.
- Before each move, check what your opponent's last move threatens.
- Count attackers and defenders before you capture.
