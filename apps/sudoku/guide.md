# Sudoku

## Goal
Fill the 9x9 grid so that every row, every column and every 3x3 box holds each digit 1 to 9 exactly once.

## Rules
- Some digits are given at the start (shown bold); they cannot be changed.
- Put a digit in any other square, change it, or erase it (X on the pad) as often as you like. Mistakes are not marked.
- The header shows SOLVED! when every square holds the right digit.
- Difficulty: Easy leaves 35 squares empty, Medium 45, Hard 52.
- A digit's key on the pad is greyed once it is on the board nine times.
- Hint fills the first empty or wrong square (reading from the top left) with the right digit and locks it.
- Your puzzle is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the cursor up and down (the hints say Up, Down); the side buttons move it left and right.
- Moving above the top row or below the bottom row reaches the New Game and Hint buttons; the side buttons switch between them.
- Confirm on an empty or own square opens the number pad: move with Left/Right, Confirm enters the digit (X erases), Back cancels.
- Confirm on Hint gives a hint. Confirm on New Game opens the difficulty choice: Left/Right choose, Confirm starts, Back cancels.
- Back exits.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a square to select it, then tap a digit (or X) on the pad.
- Tap Hint or New Game; in the difficulty box, tap Easy, Medium or Hard.

## Tips
- Look for a digit that fits only one place in a row, column or box.
- In a square, rule out the digits already in its row, column and box; if one is left, it is the answer.
- If the board is full but not SOLVED, use Hint: it fixes the first wrong square.
