# Inner Circle

## Goal

A party game for 4 to 8 players around one reader. Together, find the secret word with yes/no questions before time runs out - then unmask the Insider, the one player who secretly knew the word all along.

## Roles

- The Guide knows the secret word. They tell everyone they are the Guide and answer questions only with yes, no or "I don't know".
- The Insider also knows the word, but keeps quiet about it. They steer the questions so the word gets found in time, without being caught.
- Everyone else is one of the circle: they do not know the word and ask the questions.

The words are everyday English words (Apple, Lighthouse, Ice cream...).

## Setting up

In the menu, select "Players" to step through 4 to 8 and "Time to find the word" to step through 3, 4 or 5 minutes. Then choose "Deal the roles".

## Playing a round

1. Pass the reader round. Each player in turn presses "Show my role", reads it alone while the others look away, then presses "Hide it" and passes the reader on.
2. After the last player, the reader names the Guide. Press "Start the clock".
3. Ask the Guide yes/no questions and call out guesses. As soon as someone says the word, press "The word is found!".
4. Now you get as long again as you just used to talk it over: who seemed to know too much? When the time is up (or when you press "Time to point"), everyone points at once at who they think the Insider is.
5. Press "Reveal the Insider".

## Who wins

- If nobody finds the word in time, everyone loses - the Insider too.
- If the word is found and most players pointed at the Insider, the circle wins.
- If the word is found but the Insider escapes, the Insider wins.

## Controls

Buttons (X3/X4): Confirm presses the big button at the bottom; Back goes to the menu (Resume continues the round). In the menu, the side buttons Up/Down move and Confirm selects.

Touch (X4 Pro): tap the big button at the bottom.

## Tips

- Insider: do not find the word too fast. Ask a few broad questions that happen to point the right way.
- Guide: you are not the Insider, so you can watch the questions closely too.
- Circle: notice who jumped to the right area too quickly - or who held back.
