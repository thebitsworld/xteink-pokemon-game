# Yacht

## Goal
Score as many points as you can with five dice over 13 turns. Each turn you fill one of the 13 boxes on your scorecard. Play alone for a best score, against the device, or with a friend.

## A turn
1. Roll all five dice.
2. Hold the dice you want to keep (a held die is drawn black) and roll the rest. You may roll up to three times in all.
3. Score the dice in one empty box. You must score after your third roll, and you can score earlier.
Every box is used exactly once. If the dice do not fit any box you still need, take a zero somewhere.

## The boxes
- Ones to Sixes: the total of the dice showing that number (three 5s = 15).
- Upper bonus: 35 extra points if Ones to Sixes add up to 63 or more (the card shows your progress, for example 41/63).
- Three of a kind / Four of a kind: at least three (four) dice the same; scores the total of all five dice.
- Full house: three of one number and two of another: 25.
- Small straight: four numbers in a row (1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5 or 3-4-5-6): 30.
- Large straight: five in a row: 40.
- Yacht: all five dice the same: 50.
- Chance: any dice; scores their total.

## Extra Yachts
If you roll another Yacht after scoring 50 in the Yacht box, you get a 100-point bonus (a * appears by your total). Whenever the Yacht box is already filled, a Yacht also acts as a joker: it must go in the matching upper box if that is still free; otherwise in any lower box, where Full house, Small and Large straight pay in full.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right step through the dice, the Roll button and every scorecard row.
- The side buttons (Up/Down) jump between the dice row, Roll and the scorecard, and step through the card.
- Confirm holds or releases a die, rolls, or scores in the selected box. Possible scores are shown in brackets.
- Scoring a zero asks for a second Confirm. Back opens the menu.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a die to hold it, tap Roll to roll, tap a box to score (tap a zero box twice). Tap Menu for the menu.

## Tips for beginners
- The upper bonus is worth chasing: three of each number (3+6+9+12+15+18) gives exactly 63.
- Keep Chance for a bad turn rather than using it early.
- Go for a Yacht when you already have three or four of a kind.
- When forced to take a zero, put it in a box that is hard to fill, such as Yacht or Ones.
