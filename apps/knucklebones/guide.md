# Knucklebones

## Goal
A dice duel. Each player has a 3x3 grid: three columns of up to three dice. Your grid is at the bottom, the opponent's at the top. When a grid is full, the game ends and the higher total wins.

## Rules
- Each turn you get one die, already rolled (shown in the middle band, with an arrow pointing to the grid it goes into). Place it in one of your columns that still has room.
- Knock out: placing a die removes every die of the same value from the opponent's column directly facing it.
- Scoring: in each column, every value counts as value x count x count. So matching dice multiply: one 4 scores 4, two 4s score 16, three 4s score 36. Different values just add up.
- Each column's score is shown beside it; each player's total is in the middle band.
- The game ends the moment either player fills all nine places. Higher total wins; equal totals are a draw.
- Against the device, a coin toss decides who starts. With two players, the bottom player starts.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. Your wins, losses and draws are counted for each level.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader; the bottom player is the first one.
- Resume continues an unfinished game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right (or the side buttons) choose a column. Full columns are skipped.
- While you choose, the opponent's dice that would be knocked out are greyed.
- Confirm places the die. Back opens the menu (Back in the menu exits).
- After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap one of the columns of the player to move to place the die there.
- Tap Menu for the menu; after a game, tap anywhere to play again.

## Tips for beginners
- Stack the same value in one column: three 6s are worth 54.
- Knocking out a pair or a triple from the opponent is often worth more than the points you add.
- Do not build a big stack in a column where the opponent can easily match and wipe it out; high pairs are a target.
- Filling your grid ends the game. If you are ahead, finish quickly; if behind, keep space open.
- Put low dice where they do the least harm, ideally in a column that will knock out something.
