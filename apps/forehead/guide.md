# Forehead

## Goal
A party game for a group. One player holds the reader to their forehead, screen facing the others, and guesses the word on it from their clues. Get as many words as you can before the time runs out.

## How to play
1. Choose a category: Animals, Food & drink, Jobs, Around the house, Sports & games, Actions, Places or Things. Your best score for each is shown under it.
2. Choose the round length with the Time button: 60, 90 or 120 seconds.
3. Press Start and hold the reader to your forehead, screen facing out.
4. The others describe the word without saying it (or a part of it). Guess out loud.
5. Got it: the next word comes up and you score a point. Stuck: pass to the next word, no point.
6. When the time is up, the round ends and shows your score and every word: + for got, - for passed.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Category screen: the buttons move the selection, Confirm chooses, Back exits.
- During a round: Right or Confirm for Got it, Left to Pass, Back stops the round early.
- After a round: Confirm plays again in the same category, Back returns to the categories.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a category, the Time button, or Exit.
- During a round: tap the right half of the screen for Got it, the left half to Pass.
- After a round: tap Play again, or anywhere above it for the categories.

## Tips
- Clue givers: act it out, describe what it does, or name things that go with it.
- Pass quickly on a hard word: points count, not effort.
- Hold the reader steady so the others can read the word.
