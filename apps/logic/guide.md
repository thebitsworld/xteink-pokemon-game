# Logic Puzzles

Three pen-and-paper puzzles, made fresh on the reader. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can find it by reasoning, without guessing. Pick a puzzle, then a size. Each puzzle keeps one game in progress when you leave it (choose Continue), and counts the puzzles you solved and your best time. The clock stops while a menu is open.

## Hashi (bridges)

Each circle is an island. Its number says how many bridges end at it.

- Bridges run straight across, left-right or up-down, between two islands with nothing in between.
- Two islands can share one or two bridges, never more.
- Bridges cannot cross each other.
- When every island has its number, all islands must be joined into one network.

An island with all its bridges turns black; one with too many gets a thick ring.

## Lights Out

Pressing a light switches it and its four neighbours (on becomes off, off becomes on). Switch every light off. The moves are counted. Every board can be solved: it was made by pressing lights of a dark board.

## Slitherlink

Draw one closed loop along the dotted lines. A number tells how many sides of its square the loop runs along; squares without a number can have any.

- The loop never crosses or touches itself and has no loose ends.
- Mark a side with a cross when you are sure the loop does not go there.

A met number turns thin; one with too many lines turns white on black.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)

- Back opens the menu: Resume, Check for mistakes (counts wrong bridges, lines and crosses), Restart (clears the board, the clock keeps going), New puzzle, Other puzzles.
- Hashi: the arrows move the cursor from island to island. Confirm picks the island; then an arrow adds a bridge that way: one, two, then none again. Confirm or Back puts the island down.
- Lights Out: the arrows move the cursor, Confirm presses the light.
- Slitherlink: the cursor sits on a dot and the arrows move it along the lines. Confirm switches the pen: Move only moves; Line draws a line where you move (moving over a line rubs it out); Cross marks crosses the same way.
- After solving, Confirm starts the next puzzle.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)

- Tap Menu for the menu.
- Hashi: tap an island, then a neighbour, to add a bridge; do it again for two, a third time for none. Tapping the water between two islands does the same.
- Lights Out: tap a light.
- Slitherlink: tap a side to cycle it: line, cross, empty.
- After solving, tap the screen for the next puzzle.

## Tips

- Hashi: an island with 8 needs two bridges each way, and a 4 with only two neighbours needs two to each. Whenever a number is twice the island's neighbours, all its bridges are known.
- Hashi: two 1s cannot be joined to each other, nor two 2s with a double bridge: the pair would be cut off from the rest.
- Lights Out: work row by row. Press the light below each light still on in the row above. If lights stay on in the last row, restart and try other presses in the top row.
- Slitherlink: a 0 has crosses all round. Two 3s side by side share their middle line. Every dot has two lines or none.
