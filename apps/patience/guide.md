# Patience

Two solitaire games in one app: FreeCell and Spider. Both are played with every card face up or turning up as you go, and both are won by planning, not by luck of the draw.

## FreeCell: the goal

Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, each suit from ace up to king. Deals are numbered like the classic FreeCell program, so deal 1 here is deal 1 there. Almost every deal can be won.

## FreeCell: the rules

- The cards are dealt face up in eight cascades. Only the last card of a cascade, the one shown whole, is free to move.
- A card goes onto a card one rank higher of the other colour: a red 6 on a black 7.
- Each of the four free cells holds one card. An empty cascade takes any card.
- You may move a whole run of cards in order at once, as long as the free cells and empty cascades could carry it one card at a time: at most free cells + 1, doubled for each empty cascade. The status line shows that limit as "Max".
- Cards that can no longer be needed on the table go up to the foundations by themselves.

## Spider: the goal

Spider uses two decks (104 cards). Build complete runs from king down to ace in one suit; a finished run leaves the table. Clear all eight runs to win. Choose one suit (easy), two suits or four suits (hard).

## Spider: the rules

- Ten columns are dealt with only their top card face up. A face-down card turns over when it is uncovered.
- Any card goes onto a card one rank higher, whatever the suit. Only a run of one suit can be moved together.
- An empty column takes any card or run.
- The stock deals one more card onto every column, five times in all. As in the classic game, you cannot deal while a column is empty.

## Controls

On the X3 and X4:

1. Left and Right move the frame over the columns, then the free cells and the foundations (FreeCell) or the stock (Spider).
2. Up takes more cards of the run, Down fewer.
3. Confirm picks the cards up (they turn dark), move to where they should go and press Confirm again. Confirm on the place you picked them from sends them to the best place: a foundation, a card they fit, an empty column, then a free cell.
4. Back puts the cards down again, or opens the menu.

On the X4 Pro, tap a card to pick it up with the cards on top of it, then tap where it goes. Tap the picked cards again to send them to the best place. Undo and Menu are at the top right. The game is not moved on the first tap, so a slip of the finger costs nothing.

Undo has no limit: it takes back moves all the way to the deal, from the menu or the Undo button. Your game is kept when you leave the app. The menu shows each game's wins, games played and best winning streak; starting a new game before finishing one ends your streak.

## Tips

- In FreeCell, keep free cells empty for as long as you can, and an empty cascade is worth even more: it doubles the run you can move.
- Free the aces and twos early; low cards buried deep are the usual reason a deal gets stuck.
- In Spider, build on cards of the same suit whenever you can, since only same-suit runs can move together.
- Before dealing from the Spider stock, tidy up and fill the empty columns: the new cards cover everything.
