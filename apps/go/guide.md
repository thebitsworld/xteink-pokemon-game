# Go

## Goal
Surround more of the board than your opponent. At the end, each side scores one point for every stone it has on the board plus every empty point that is walled in by its stones only. White gets 7.5 extra points (komi) for moving second, so there are no draws.

## Rules
- Black moves first. On your turn, place a stone on an empty point (where lines cross) or pass.
- Liberties are the empty points right next to a stone, up, down, left or right. Stones of one colour that touch along lines form a group and share their liberties.
- A group with no liberties left is captured and taken off the board.
- You may not play a stone that leaves your own group without liberties, unless it captures something (shown as "Not allowed there").
- Ko: if a single stone captures a single stone, the opponent may not capture straight back on the next move. Play elsewhere first.
- Two passes in a row end the game.
- Dead stones are not removed by agreement: they count for their owner. Capture any enemy stones inside your area before you pass.
- Captured stones are shown on screen but do not add points; only stones on the board and surrounded areas count. After the game, small squares show whose area each empty point is.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. You always play Black.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Board: 9x9 or 13x13 for the next game.
- Resume continues an unfinished game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the cursor along a line; the side buttons (Up/Down) move it between lines. Below the bottom line is the Pass button.
- Confirm plays a stone, or passes when Pass is selected.
- Back opens the menu (Back in the menu exits). After a game, Confirm plays again.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a point to play there; tap Pass to pass; tap Menu for the menu.
- After a game, tap anywhere to play again.

## Tips for beginners
- Corners are easiest to claim, then the edges, then the centre.
- A group needs two separate eyes (enclosed empty points) to live for good.
- Watch for atari: a group with only one liberty left. Save yours, capture theirs.
- When the device passes, check that no enemy stones are still alive inside your area, then pass too.
