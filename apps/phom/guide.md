# Phom

## Goal
Phỏm (also called tá lả) is the Vietnamese rummy. You play against three device players: East, North and West. Build melds, throw away your loose high cards, and at the end hold the fewest points outside your melds.

## Cards and melds
- One deck of 52 cards. The ace is low: A 2 3 ... 10 J Q K.
- Points: A is 1, 2 to 10 count their number, J 11, Q 12, K 13.
- A meld (phỏm) is three or four cards of the same rank, such as 7 7 7, or three or more cards of one suit in a row, such as 4 5 6 of hearts.
- A run does not wrap: Q K A and K A 2 are not melds.

## The deal
- The first player gets 10 cards, the others 9; the other 15 cards are the stock. The first player is chosen at random for the first hand, then it is the winner of the last hand.
- Turns go You, East, North, West. The first player starts by throwing a card.

## Your turn
1. Take a card: either eat the card the previous player just threw, or draw the top card of the stock.
2. Throw one card face up.
- You may eat a card only when it makes a meld with cards in your hand. An eaten card stays in that meld: you cannot throw it, nor a card its meld needs.
- Each eaten card needs a meld of its own: two eaten cards never count in the same meld.
- Eaten cards lie face up in front of the player (a thick frame on the table, a black dot on your own cards).

## The end of a hand
- The hand ends when everyone has thrown four cards (four rounds). If nobody ate, the stock runs out just then.
- Everyone lays down their melds; the game picks the split that leaves the fewest points. The cards outside melds are counted.
- Fewest points wins. A player with no meld at all is móm and comes last. On equal points, the player who threw first in the hand ranks higher.
- Points table: 3 for 1st, 2 for 2nd, 1 for 3rd, 0 for 4th; móm scores 0.

## Ù
A player holding 10 cards (just after eating or drawing, or the first player at the deal) with at least 9 of them in melds goes ù and wins at once: 5 points, nobody else scores.

## Rules left out
No money or chips, no bonus for eating the last card of a round (chốt), no laying off cards onto other players' melds (gửi), no penalties (đền) and no ù khan.

## Levels
Normal plays sensibly but is sometimes careless. Hard remembers every card thrown and eaten, drops pairs that can no longer be completed, and on its last throw avoids feeding the next player.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the cursor (the bar under a card) along your hand and on to the Eat, Draw and Discard buttons.
- Confirm picks the card under the cursor (it stands raised) or presses the button. A button is black when you may use it.
- Side buttons: Down draws, or throws the picked card; Up eats.
- Your hand is sorted with its melds first, each underlined. Back opens the menu; a hand in progress is kept, also when you exit.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a card to pick it, then tap Discard. Tap Eat or Draw to take a card.
- Tap Menu at the top right for the menu. After a hand, tap anywhere for the next one.

## Tips
- Throw high loose cards early: a king left at the end costs 13 points.
- Keep pairs and near runs (such as 5 and 7 of a suit) while you still have cards to take.
- Watch what the next player throws: cards like those are safer to give.
