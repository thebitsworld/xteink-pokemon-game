# Whodunit

## Goal

A logic-grid murder mystery, made fresh every time. Each suspect had exactly one weapon and was in exactly one place (on Hard, also had one motive), and no two suspects share any of them. Work out who had what and was where, find the murderer, and accuse them with the right weapon and place.

Every case has exactly one answer and can be solved by reasoning alone - no guessing.

## Levels

- Easy: 3 suspects, weapons and places.
- Medium: 4 suspects, weapons and places.
- Hard: 4 suspects, weapons, places and motives.

## The three tabs

### Clues

The numbered clues, then the murder clue in bold ("The body was found beside a broken teacup."), then the Case file. Clues come in a few forms:

- "Baron Cobb was in the library" or "was not in the attic or the kitchen".
- "Whoever had the brick was in the greenhouse or the attic" (one of two).
- Trait clues: "Whoever was in the attic was tall" or "had a weapon that was heavy". Look in the Case file to see which suspects are tall or which weapons are heavy.

The Case file lists each suspect's traits (left- or right-handed, tall or short, eye colour), each weapon's (heavy or light, metal or not) and each place's (indoors or outdoors, and a detail such as "next to a stopped clock"). The murder clue's detail tells you the place of the murder; whoever was there is the murderer.

### Grid

A staircase of blocks, one for each pair of categories. Each cell cycles: blank, cross (no), circle (yes), blank. A circle also crosses out the rest of its row and column in that block. The pair under the cursor is named below the grid.

### Accuse

Choose a suspect, weapon and place (and motive on Hard), then press Accuse. You get one accusation: right or wrong, the case ends and the answer is shown. The clock only runs while the case is open.

## Controls

Buttons (X3/X4): on the tab row, Left/Right switch tabs and Confirm or the side button Down opens one. Inside a tab:

- Clues: Down, Right or Confirm turn to the next page; Up or Left to the previous one (Up on the first page goes back to the tabs).
- Grid: the arrows move the cursor; Confirm marks the cell. Up from the top row goes back to the tabs.
- Accuse: Up/Down choose a line, Left/Right change it, Confirm on Accuse accuses.

Back opens the menu; your marks and time are kept.

Touch (X4 Pro): tap the tabs (Menu is the last one). On Clues, tap the right half for the next page, the left half for the previous one. On the Grid, tap a cell to cycle it. On Accuse, tap the left or right half of a line to change it, then tap Accuse.

## Tips

- Mark every "no" first; when a row in a block has one blank left, it is a yes.
- Carry facts across blocks: if Hazel had the rope and the rope was in the attic, Hazel was in the attic.
- Solve the place block early: the murder clue only needs the place.
