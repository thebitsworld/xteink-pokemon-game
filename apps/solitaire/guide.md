# Solitaire

## Goal
Klondike solitaire: move all 52 cards onto the four foundations (top right), one pile per suit, from ace up to king.

## Rules
- The tableau has seven columns. Only the face-up cards can be moved; when a column's last face-up card leaves, the card under it turns over.
- In a column, build down in alternating colours: a red 6 on a black 7. You can move a face-up run together.
- Only a king (or a run starting with a king) can go into an empty column (marked K).
- Foundations start with an ace (slots marked A) and go up in the same suit.
- The stock (top left) deals onto the waste: one card at a time in Draw 1, three in Draw 3. Only the top waste card can be played. When the stock is empty, take it again to turn the waste back over; there is no limit.
- Red suits are drawn hollow, black suits solid.
- Once every card is face up and the stock is used up, the game finishes itself.
- Your game is kept when you exit. Undo goes back up to 20 moves (not across an exit).

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right choose a column. Up/Down (the side buttons) move between the top row and the tableau, and within a column choose which face-up card to take.
- Confirm on the stock deals. Elsewhere Confirm picks up the card (or run); move to the target and Confirm again to place it.
- Confirm twice on the same card sends it wherever it fits, foundations first.
- Back cancels a pick, otherwise opens the menu (New game, Undo last move, Exit).

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap the stock to deal. Tap a card to pick it up, then tap where it should go; tap it again to send it wherever it fits.
- Undo and Menu are at the top right.

## Tips
- Turning over face-down cards matters most: play from the tallest columns first.
- Do not empty a column unless you have a king ready for it.
