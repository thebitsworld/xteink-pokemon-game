# Bazaar

## Goal

You and the device are rival traders. Collect goods, sell them in sets for rupee tokens, and keep more camels than your rival. The richer trader at the end of a round wins a seal; the first to two seals wins the match.

## Setup

The deck has 55 cards: 6 Diamond, 6 Gold, 6 Silver, 8 Cloth, 8 Spice, 10 Leather and 11 Camels. The Market always shows 5 cards and starts with 3 camels. Each trader gets 5 cards; camels go straight into your herd, the rest into your hand. Your hand holds at most 7 goods; camels do not count.

## A turn

On your turn do exactly one of these:

- Take one good from the Market (the deck refills its place). Not allowed with 7 goods in hand.
- Take all the camels from the Market (the deck refills their places).
- Exchange: take two or more goods from the Market and give back the same number of cards from your hand and/or camels from your herd. You cannot give back a kind you are taking, and the deck is not touched.
- Sell any number of one kind of good from your hand. Diamond, Gold and Silver must be sold at least two at a time.

## Selling and tokens

Each good has a pile of tokens, best first, so selling early pays more:

- Diamond 7 7 5 5 5, Gold 6 6 5 5 5, Silver 5 5 5 5 5
- Cloth and Spice 5 3 3 2 2 1 1, Leather 4 3 2 1 1 1 1 1 1

You take one token per card sold; when a pile is sold out, extra cards earn nothing. Selling 3 cards adds a bonus token (1-3), 4 cards a bigger one (4-6), 5 or more the biggest (8-10).

## End of a round

A round ends when three token piles are sold out, or when the deck cannot refill the Market. The trader with more camels then gets 5 rupees. The richer trader wins a seal. Ties go to more bonus tokens, then more goods tokens, then more camels. The loser of a round starts the next one.

## Controls

Pick cards first, then press a button: Take, Sell or Clear.

- Market card: selects it. Picking one camel selects all the camels.
- A kind in your hand: the first press selects all of it, each further press one fewer, then none.
- The camel slot (last in your hand row): each press gives back one more camel.
- Take: one good, all the camels, or an exchange, depending on what is selected. Sell: one kind from your hand.

Buttons (X3/X4): the side buttons Up/Down move between the Market, your hand and the buttons; Left/Right move along a row; Confirm picks or presses; Back opens the menu. After a round, Confirm goes on.

Touch (X4 Pro): tap cards and buttons; tap Menu at the top right. After a round, tap anywhere to go on.

## Tips

- Camels are free hand space for exchanges, and the herd bonus is worth 5.
- Taking camels refills the Market from the deck: it may hand your rival something good.
- Selling small sets early grabs the high tokens; waiting for 4 or 5 gets a bigger bonus. Watch the piles.
- Do not leave two precious goods in the Market for the device.
