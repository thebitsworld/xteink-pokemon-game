# Dots and Boxes

## Goal
Take more boxes than your opponent. The board is a grid of dots; players take turns drawing a line between two neighbouring dots. Whoever draws the fourth side of a box owns it.

## Rules
- On your turn, draw one free line, across or down, between two dots next to each other.
- If your line closes a box, the box is yours and you must draw again. One line can close two boxes at once.
- If your line closes no box, the turn passes.
- The game ends when every line is drawn. Most boxes wins; an even board can end in a draw.
- Your boxes are filled solid. The other player's boxes show a cross. The lines of the last turn are drawn thicker, with a white stripe, so you can see what just happened.

## Starting a game
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. You are player 1 (solid boxes); who starts alternates from game to game.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Board: 3x3, 4x4, 5x5 or 6x6 boxes. The size applies to the next game.
- Take back a move undoes your last line (and the device's lines after it).
- Resume continues the game; it is kept when you exit.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left, Right, Up, Down: move the cursor to the nearest free line that way. Repeated presses zigzag along the row or column.
- Confirm: draw the line under the cursor.
- Back: open the menu (the game is kept).
- When the game is over, Confirm starts another one.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap a line between two dots to draw it. The nearest free line to your finger is taken, if it is close enough.
- Menu at the top right opens the menu.

## Tips
- Never draw the third side of a box early: your opponent closes it and draws again.
- Late in the game every line gives something away. Give away the shortest chain of boxes you can.
- A chain of two can be handed over by its middle line: your opponent then cannot hand it back.
- The strongest trick is the double-cross: when you are handed a long chain, take all but its last two boxes and give those two away with the far line. Your opponent takes them but then has to open the next chain for you.
- Count the long chains (three boxes or more) before the safe lines run out: who has to open the first one usually loses.
