# Mancala

## Goal
Mancala is a family of sowing games played all over the world. This app plays Kalah, the version most people know. Move seeds around the board into your store. When the game ends, the player with more seeds in their store wins.

## The board
- Two rows of six pits. The bottom row is yours (in two-player games, the bottom player's); the top row belongs to the device or the other player.
- Each player has a store at their right-hand end: yours is the tall pit at the right of the screen, the device's the one at the left.
- Every pit starts with 4 seeds (3, 5 or 6 with the Seeds per pit option). Seeds are drawn as dots, a number past 10, and every count is also printed next to the board.
- Pits are numbered 1 to 6 from the left of the screen, in both rows.

## A turn
1. Pick one of your pits that has seeds and take them all.
2. Sow them one by one into the next pits counter-clockwise: along your row to the right, into your store, then along the other row to the left. You skip the other player's store.
3. If the last seed lands in your own store, you move again (an extra turn). Extra turns can follow one another.
4. If the last seed lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit facing it has seeds, you capture both: that seed and everything facing it go to your store.

## The end
- When all six pits on either side are empty after a move, the game ends. The player who still has seeds on their side puts them all into their own store.
- The fuller store wins. Equal stores are a draw.

## Options
- Seeds per pit: 3, 4 (the usual), 5 or 6. More seeds make longer games.
- Empty capture (off by default): when on, a last seed in an empty pit of yours is captured on its own even if the facing pit is empty. Most rule books do not do this; some do.
- You play first or second: who opens the next game against the device.
- Options apply to the next new game; a game in progress keeps its own.

## Playing
- vs Device: Easy, Normal or Hard. Easy sometimes plays at random, Normal looks four moves ahead, Hard thinks deeper within about a second and a half. Your wins and losses are shown under each level.
- Two players: take turns on the same reader.
- Take back a move (menu): against the device, it goes back to before your last move.
- A game in progress is kept when you leave the app (Resume).
- After a move, an arrow marks the pit it started from, changed pits are framed, captured pits shaded, and the lines under the board say what happened.

## Controls (X3/X4 buttons)
- Left/Right move the pointer along your row (only pits with seeds).
- Confirm sows that pit.
- Back opens the menu. When a game is over, Confirm starts the next one.

## Controls (X4 Pro touch)
- Tap one of your pits to sow it.
- Tap Menu (top right) for the menu. After a game, tap the screen to play again.

## Tips
- A pit holding exactly as many seeds as its distance to your store gives an extra turn. Play the pit nearest the store first: the others still line up afterwards.
- Watch the empty pits on the other side: a pit facing a big pile is a capture waiting to happen.
- Near the end, count the seeds: if one side runs out, the other player keeps everything left on their own side.
